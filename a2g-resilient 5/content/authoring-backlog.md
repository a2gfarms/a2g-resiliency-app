# Air2Ground Resilient — Core Authoring Backlog

*The complete catalog of authored "core" actions. This is the whole board, not the launch list. Items marked ★ are the recommended Minimum Lovable Set for launch — enough to feel complete, small enough to author in a focused sprint and react to before anyone pays.*

**Legend**
- ★ = recommended launch core
- **Profiles** = how many authored variants the action needs (Apartment / Suburb / Rural). "Neutral" = one core, AI adapts at runtime.
- **Flags** = constraints the core must explicitly address (HOA/lease, alpha-gal/AGS, no outdoor space, limited time, mobility, budget floor).
- ⚠ **Verify** = carries real accuracy/safety risk; needs a food-safety source, medical note, or your lived experience confirmed before it goes in front of a paying customer.

---

## How each core is structured (the template every item below fills)

Every authored core has the same skeleton — this is what we lock with the first one:

1. **Title** — plain, action-first ("Store a 2-week water reserve").
2. **Why this matters for you** — the AI-personalized opener (we author a *neutral* version + the profile hooks the AI keys off).
3. **Meta** — time to do, the concrete goal/target, difficulty.
4. **Constraint callout** — the lease-safe / AGS-aware / no-yard version. Authored per relevant flag.
5. **The steps** — the fixed, authored, on-brand core. 4–7 checkable steps. This is the moat.
6. **What you'll need** — supply list, authored at 2–3 budget tiers (honest cheap path first, upgrade path noted).
7. **Coach starter questions** — 3 seeded "ask the coach" chips per profile that show off contextual help.
8. **Score impact** — which pillar(s) completing this raises, and by how much.

## PILLAR 1 — WATER

**★ 1.1 Store a 2-week water reserve**
Profiles: Apt / Suburb / Rural (3). Flags: no-space, lease, budget floor, mobility (lifting). ⚠ Verify: storage life, treatment/preserver dosing, container food-grade ratings.
The flagship template — start here. Apartment closet kit → household barrel + rotation → rural cistern/large-volume.

**★ 1.2 Make questionable water safe to drink**
Profiles: Neutral (1, AI adapts scale). Flags: health (immunocompromised). ⚠⚠ Verify HEAVILY: boiling times, filtration vs. purification distinction, chemical treatment ratios, what filters do/don't remove. This is a "wrong info gets someone sick" core — needs a cited authoritative source (CDC/EPA-level) and a clear "this is not for chemical/industrial contamination" boundary.

1.3 Build a simple water rotation habit
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: limited time. Low risk. The "keep it fresh without thinking" follow-on to 1.1.

1.4 Capture and use rainwater (where legal)
Profiles: Suburb / Rural (2). Flags: HOA, **legality varies by state** ⚠ Verify legality framing. Skip for apartment. A "next level" action, not launch.

## PILLAR 2 — FOOD

**★ 2.1 Build a rotating deep pantry**
Profiles: Apt / Suburb / Rural (3). Flags: no-space (single shelf version), budget, AGS (protein choices). Low-moderate risk. The second flagship — pairs with water as the "week one" foundation. Apartment = one shelf FIFO; suburb/rural = 30-day buffer scaling up.

**★ 2.2 Stock a resilient pantry around alpha-gal**
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: **AGS core** ⚠ Verify: this is a health core — needs a medical/dietitian sanity check on hidden mammal-derived ingredients (gelatin, tallow, carmine, magnesium stearate questions, broth bases). HIGH value to your specific community; HIGH responsibility. Shelley's voice leads.

**★ 2.3 Learn 3 no-fail shelf-stable meals**
Profiles: Neutral (1), beginner-targeted. Flags: limited skill, AGS variants. Low risk (recipes, not preservation). Confidence-builder for the "zero skills" user — the emotional on-ramp to the whole app.

2.4 Start a windowsill / small-space food garden
Profiles: Apt (1), extends to Suburb. Flags: lease, no-space, north-facing light. Low risk. High delight, fast win. Strong launch candidate if sprint has room.

2.5 Plant an HOA-friendly resilience garden
Profiles: Suburb (1). Flags: **HOA core**, zone-dependent. Moderate risk (zone/timing accuracy). ⚠ Verify planting guidance is zone-aware or clearly tells user to localize.

2.6 Safely freeze and store meat you've bought in bulk
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: AGS (n/a for storage), power (ties to outage core). ⚠⚠ Verify: freezer temps, safe storage durations, thaw-safety, refreeze rules, "when is it no longer safe" after an outage. Food-safety source required. *Natural A2G tie-in to the Meats business — but accuracy is non-negotiable here.*

2.7 Preserve food without specialized equipment (intro)
Profiles: Neutral (1). ⚠⚠⚠ Verify HEAVILY: any canning/preservation guidance is a botulism-risk domain. I would **not** author detailed canning for launch. If included at all, scope tightly to fridge-pickling/freezing/drying with explicit "follow tested recipes" guardrails and a pointer to authoritative sources. Flag for later, with expert review.

## PILLAR 3 — POWER

**★ 3.1 Tiered power backup for outages**
Profiles: Apt / Suburb / Rural (3). Flags: no-space (no generator), budget, mobility, medical (powered medical devices) ⚠ Verify: the medical-device dependency note must be careful — direct people to their provider, never improvise. Apartment = power station + lights; suburb = station→generator path; rural = whole-home/solar mention.

3.2 Keep food cold when the power's out
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: ties to 2.6. ⚠ Verify: the "4-hour fridge / 48-hour full freezer" safety rules and the "when to throw it out" thresholds. Food-safety source.

3.3 Light and charge without the grid
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: budget, fire-safety (candles vs. battery). ⚠ Verify: any fuel/flame/CO guidance — generator-indoors and CO poisoning is a kills-people topic, must be unambiguous.

3.4 Stay warm / cool safely during an outage
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: health, mobility, **CO and heat-source safety** ⚠⚠ Verify: indoor heating safety is a fatality domain. Careful authoring + source.

## PILLAR 4 — SUPPLIES & SAFETY

**★ 4.1 Map your home shutoffs & exits**
Profiles: Apt / Suburb / Rural (light variants). Flags: none major. **Lowest risk, fastest win, free.** Excellent launch core — builds the habit muscle, costs the user nothing, near-zero verify burden. Great candidate for the *very first thing* a new user does.

**★ 4.2 Build a grab-and-go bag**
Profiles: Neutral (1), scales by household. Flags: health (meds buffer), mobility, AGS (food choices), pets. Low-moderate risk. Classic, expected, gives immediate sense of progress.

4.3 Assemble a real first-aid kit (and know the basics)
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: health, AGS (some products). ⚠⚠ Verify: first-aid *instruction* is medical content — author the *kit* confidently, but keep any *treatment* guidance minimal and point to a Red Cross-level source / a course. Don't let the app play doctor.

4.4 A simple household emergency plan
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: mobility, kids, pets, elderly dependents. Low risk. The "get everyone on the same page" core. Pairs with 4.1.

4.5 Important documents & cash-on-hand readiness
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: privacy ⚠ (we tell people to secure docs — be careful never to collect them). Low risk if framed as guidance only. Note: per our own rules, the app *coaches*, it never *stores* sensitive documents or financial data.

4.6 Two weeks of essential medications & health supplies
Profiles: Neutral (1). Flags: **health core**, AGS. ⚠⚠ Verify: never advise on stockpiling prescriptions beyond what a provider authorizes — frame as "talk to your pharmacist/provider about a buffer." Medical boundary.

## Authoring cost reality (so the sprint is plannable)

- **Neutral cores** (one version, AI adapts): fastest. ~14 of the 27.
- **3-profile cores** (Apt/Suburb/Rural): ~3× the writing. Only 4 truly need all three: 1.1, 2.1, 3.1, and partially 4.1.
- **⚠ Verify cores**: the gate isn't writing speed, it's the review pass. The heavy ones (1.2, 2.6, 2.7, 3.3, 3.4, 4.3, 4.6) need a real source or expert eye before publish. **2.7 (canning) I'd hold entirely for a later, expert-reviewed release.**

So the launch-set authoring load is really: 3 full-profile cores, 6 neutral cores, of which 3 need a careful verify pass (1.2, 2.2, 3.1). That's a sprint-sized chunk — not a year of writing.
